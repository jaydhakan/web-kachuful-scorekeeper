# Emerald Gilt deck art

The card back, the Queen of hearts and the Kings of spades, diamonds and clubs come from the emerald-and-gold
playing-card deck the Emerald Gilt theme is based on: photographs of the cards, redrawn flat and clean from those
photographs (the artwork itself unchanged). They are used with the rights holder's permission (held in writing
outside this repository) for this app and this public repository only: not in the share image, the app icon, the
splash screen or the manifest.

Replace each file with the publisher's original when it arrives (600×840 WebP, quality about 75; `back-mini.webp`
is 96×136, twice the size of the small dealt cards). Never use another suit's card in place of a missing one.

`standins/` is not deck art: our own drawings (ivory face, gold rule, outlined rank, pip) for every card the deck
art lacks, plus a back, from the Baithak round-2 kit. They may appear anywhere, share images included.
