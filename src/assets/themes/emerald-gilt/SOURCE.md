# Emerald Gilt deck art

The card back, the Queen of hearts and the King of clubs are photographs of the emerald-and-gold playing-card deck
the Emerald Gilt theme is based on, straightened and cropped. They are used with the rights holder's permission
(held in writing outside this repository) for this app and this public repository only: not in the share image,
the app icon, the splash screen or the manifest.

Replace each file with the publisher's original when it arrives (600×840 WebP, quality about 85; `back-mini.webp`
is 96×136, twice the size of the small dealt cards). The King of spades and the King of diamonds have no file yet,
so those trumps show the suit symbol. Never use another suit's card in their place.
