# Kaali Teedi (Black 3) — Official House Rules

## 1. Players, Decks & Setup
* **1 Deck (52 Cards):** Used for **4 to 6 players** (Total Deck Points = **250**).
* **2 Decks (104 Cards):** Used for **7 to 10 players** (Total Deck Points = **500**).
* **Equal Dealing:** Remove low zero-point cards ($2$s, non-spade $3$s, or $4$s) before dealing so that every player receives an exact equal number of cards.
* **Duplicate Cards (2-Deck Games):** If two identical cards are played in the same trick (e.g., two Aces of Hearts), the **first one played** holds seniority and wins the trick (unless beaten by a higher card or trump).

---

## 2. Card Point Values
Only the cards listed below carry points. All other cards ($2, 3$ of Hearts/Diamonds/Clubs, $4, 6, 7, 8, 9$) are worth $0$ points.

| Card | Points Per Card | 1-Deck Total | 2-Deck Total | Special Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3 of Spades** (*Kaali Teedi*) | $30$ pts | $30$ pts | $60$ pts | Point card only; follows normal Spade rank ($3 > 2$). |
| **A, K, Q, J, 10** (All Suits) | $10$ pts | $200$ pts | $400$ pts | $20$ cards per deck ($50$ pts per suit). |
| **5** (All Suits) | $5$ pts | $20$ pts | $40$ pts | $4$ cards per deck. |
| **Total Points** | | **250 pts** | **500 pts** | |

---

## 3. Bidding Rules
* **1-Deck Minimum Bid:** Starts at **155 points** (increments of $5$, up to $250$).
* **2-Deck Minimum Bid:** Starts at **355 points** (increments of $5$, up to $500$).
* **Setting the Round:** The highest bidder declares the **Trump Suit (*Hukum*)** and calls out the secret partner cards (*Bheru*).

---

## 4. Team Formations & Secret Partners (*Bheru*)
The bidder calls specific cards to select secret partners. For odd player counts, the bidder's team always has one fewer player than the defending team.

| Total Players | Decks Used | Partner Cards Called (*Bheru*) | Team Split (Bidder vs. Defenders) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4 Players** | 1 Deck | $1$ Card | $2$ vs $2$ |
| **5 Players** | 1 Deck | $1$ Card | $2$ vs $3$ |
| **6 Players** | 1 Deck | $2$ Cards | $3$ vs $3$ |
| **7 Players** | 2 Decks | $2$ Cards | $3$ vs $4$ |
| **8 Players** | 2 Decks | $3$ Cards | $4$ vs $4$ |
| **9 Players** | 2 Decks | $3$ Cards | $4$ vs $5$ |
| **10 Players** | 2 Decks | $4$ Cards | $5$ vs $5$ |

* **Secret Identity:** Partners must keep their allegiance secret until the exact called card is played in a trick.
* **2-Deck Calling:** In a 2-deck game, the bidder must specify which copy of a card is the partner (e.g., *"First played Ace of Spades"* or *"Second played King of Hearts"*).

---

## 5. Gameplay & Trick Rules
1. **First Lead:** The highest bidder leads the opening trick.
2. **Following Suit:** Players must play a card of the suit that was led if they have one.
3. **Cutting / Discarding:** If a player has no cards of the led suit, they may play a **Trump (*Hukum*)** card to cut and win the trick, or discard any unwanted card from another suit.
4. **Winning a Trick:** 
   * The highest **Trump** card played wins the trick.
   * If no trumps are played, the highest card of the **led suit** wins ($A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2$).
   * **Note on 3 of Spades:** The $3$ of Spades does **not** have supreme trump power. It acts as a standard Spade of rank $3$ (only beating the $2$ of Spades).

---

## 6. Scoring System (Zero-Minus Format)
Nobody ever receives negative points. Points are only added to the winning side of each round.

| Round Outcome | Bidder Gets | Each Partner Gets | Each Defender Gets |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bidder Team Wins** (Scores $\ge$ Bid) | $+$Bid Amount | $+$Bid Amount | $0$ |
| **Bidder Team Loses** (Scores $<$ Bid) | $0$ | $0$ | $+$Bid Amount |
| **Double Role Win** (1 player holds 2 called cards) | $+$Bid Amount | $+2\times$ Bid Amount | $0$ |
| **Double Role Loss** (1 player holds 2 called cards) | $0$ | $0$ | $+$Bid Amount |
| **Clean Sweep** (One team captures all $250/500$ pts) | $+250$ or $+500$ (if won) | $+250$ or $+500$ (if won) | $+250$ or $+500$ (if won) |

### Special Scoring Rules
* **Multi-Slot / Double Points Bonus:** If a single player holds $2$ of the called partner cards (or if the bidder holds a called card themselves) and the bidding team wins, that player receives **double the bid amount ($2\times$)** for filling two team spots (or **$3\times$** if holding $3$ called cards). If the team loses, they still receive **$0$**.
* **Clean Sweep (*Court / Washout*):** If either the Bidding Team or the Defending Team captures every single point in the deck (**$250$ points** in 1 deck, or **$500$ points** in 2 decks), every member of that winning team is awarded the full **$+250$** (1 deck) or **$+500$** (2 decks), while the opposing team gets **$0$**.
