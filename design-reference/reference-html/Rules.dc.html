How to play

Seating and dealing

1Sit in a circle.
2The dealer deals one card at a time, starting with the player on their left.
3The deal passes left each round.

Card sequence

Start at 3 cards and climb to the maximum, then come back down. One cycle is 16 rounds with one deck.

Trump order

Trump changes every round, then starts again at spades.

Bidding and the dealer rule

Everyone predicts how many tricks they will win, starting with the player after the dealer. The dealer bids last and can't make the bids add up to the cards dealt.
