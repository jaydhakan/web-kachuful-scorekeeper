What this is

A sketch of every moment in docs/WOW-MOMENTS-PLAN.md §4, with the plan's real timings and curves. Colours are a stand-in for the Sunset Glass theme; the app uses its theme tokens and real components.

Each moment plays once when you scroll to it. Use 0.25× to judge feel, Reduced motion to see the still versions (nothing is lost, only movement), and Sound for the synthesised sounds. Your device asks for reduced motion; turn it on here to match.

One grammar: the card, the stamp, the light

  • The card: big news is dealt and turns over (rotateY, 450ms, the trump turn's timing).
  • The stamp: verdicts stamp (scale 1.6 → 1, −8°, 360ms, a 3px settle): SET, BOSTON, SOLD, TRAPPED, 1000.
  • The light: winners get one gold sheen (800ms), never a loop.

Three tiers

  • Tick ≤ 300ms: every play and bid. All exist; nothing new.
  • Beat 0.8–1.6s: in place on the table; play carries on.
  • Moment ≤ 2.5s: may take the screen; tap skips; at most two a hand.
