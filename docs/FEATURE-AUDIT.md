# Feature audit

Date: 2026-11-02 · Based on reading the code on `main` at `b476586`. I did not run the app for this audit, so timings and reachability come from the code, not from a device.

Effort scale: **S** = under half a day · **M** = 1–2 days · **L** = 3+ days or touches game rules / saved data.

---

## 1. What the product is

**Kachuful** is a scorekeeper for _Judgement_ (also called Kachuful or Oh Hell), the trick-taking card game where each player bids how many tricks they'll win. Points come only from hitting the bid exactly: `max(bid, 1) × 10` for a made bid, the same amount negative for a miss ([src/domain/scoring.ts:10](../src/domain/scoring.ts#L10)).

**Who it's for:** a group at a real card table, usually friends or family who play regularly. One phone or tablet lies on the table and one person keeps score. The app handles everything people usually get wrong on paper: who deals, how many cards this round, which suit is trump, and the dealer's "hook" (the dealer can't bid a number that makes the total bids equal the cards).

**Two modes:**

| Mode                    | Who          | What it adds                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signed out (default)    | Anyone       | Everything on the device, fully offline, installable PWA. History, boards, awards and Wrapped from this device's games.                   |
| Signed in (invite only) | Jay's circle | Games sync to the FastAPI backend, a global leaderboard, an activity feed, photos, live/recap links, continuing a game on another device. |

**Two UIs:** a phone shell ([src/components/PhoneShell.tsx](../src/components/PhoneShell.tsx)) and a separate large-screen "desk" shell for 768px+ ([src/screens/desk/DeskShell.tsx](../src/screens/desk/DeskShell.tsx)). They share the reducer and domain but have their own screens.

### 2d. Structural overlap (not one feature)

- **Two full UIs.** Phone screens are about 2,960 lines; desk screens are about 3,700 lines. Setup, Summary and Winner each exist twice ([Step1](../src/screens/setup/Step1.tsx)–[Step3](../src/screens/setup/Step3.tsx) vs [DeskSetup](../src/screens/desk/DeskSetup.tsx), [Winner](../src/screens/Winner.tsx) vs [DeskWinner](../src/screens/desk/DeskWinner.tsx)). The logic is shared, but features already drift apart: the rank cards, the summary chart and the menu entry from Home are desk-only. Every new feature costs roughly double.
- **Seven ways to celebrate.** Streak overlay, badge pops, the Today strip, Awards, Achievements, the Winner's highlights and the Winner's achievement reel. Each is fine alone. Together they spread one idea over seven places.

### 3.3 Priority: what to do first

1. **R1: fix the sign-out dialogs.** Small, and it prevents accidental data loss.
2. **R2 + A1: move Start fresh and add a Menu to phone Home.** One small change that fixes a dangerous placement and four unreachable features.
3. **R3: optional email at setup.** The biggest friction at the table, and most of the backend plumbing already exists. Confirm the contract first.
4. **R4: quick play / fewer blocking animations.** Saves real time every round for regular groups.
5. **A3: backup for signed-out users.** The quiet risk: one browser clean-up wipes everything.
6. **R6: one place to manage players.** Removes a duplicate merge flow before it causes a bad merge.
7. **A2, A5, A6, R7, R8: phone parity and making finished features findable.** Cheap wins once the above are done.
8. **A4: add or remove a player mid-game.** The most valuable new feature, but it's L-sized and touches rules and saved data, so do it deliberately, tests first.

Everything in R9, A7, A8 and A9 can ride along with whichever change touches the same files.
